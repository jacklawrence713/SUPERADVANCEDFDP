Dynasty Fantasy Football Glossary

Dynasty League — a fantasy football league where you keep your entire roster year over year. Superflex (SF) — a league format with an extra flex spot that can start a QB, dramatically increasing QB values. TE Premium (TEP) — bonus points per reception for tight ends. IDP — Individual Defensive Player scoring, where you draft and start real NFL defenders. FAAB — Free Agent Acquisition Budget, a dollar amount used to bid on waiver wire players. KTC — KeepTradeCut, a crowdsourced dynasty trade value tool. ADP — Average Draft Position. Trade Value — a numerical score representing a player's dynasty worth. Age Curve — the relationship between a player's age and expected production. Taxi Squad — a developmental roster spot for young players. PUP List — Physically Unable to Perform, an NFL designation for injured players. Commissioner's Exempt List — an NFL designation preventing a player from playing while under investigation.
