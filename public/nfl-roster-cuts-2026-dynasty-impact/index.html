NFL Roster Cuts 2026 — Dynasty Fantasy Football Impact Analysis

Every NFL preseason brings a wave of roster cuts that reshapes dynasty fantasy football leagues. The 2026 roster cutdowns have been especially impactful, with several high-profile players losing their jobs and creating ripple effects across depth charts. Understanding how these moves affect dynasty trade values and waiver wire priorities is crucial for staying ahead of your league mates. Here's a breakdown of the most significant cuts and what they mean for your dynasty roster.

Will Levis — Cut by Tennessee Titans

The Titans' decision to move on from Will Levis is one of the most significant quarterback moves of the 2026 preseason. Levis showed flashes of elite arm talent but was ultimately too inconsistent and turnover-prone to hold the starting job. For dynasty managers who invested a mid-round rookie pick in Levis, this is a devastating blow. His dynasty value has cratered, and unless he lands in a favorable backup situation with a clear path to starting, he's a roster cut candidate in most dynasty formats. In Superflex leagues, he may still hold minimal stash value — but don't expect the upside you once drafted him for.

Trey Benson — Waived by Arizona Cardinals

Trey Benson's release from Arizona signals that the Cardinals are committed to their current backfield configuration. Benson was a popular dynasty stash as a physical, between-the-tackles runner, but he never carved out a consistent role. His waiver status means he'll likely land on another team's practice squad or as a depth signing. For dynasty purposes, Benson is droppable in all but the deepest leagues. Monitor his landing spot — if he ends up on a team with an aging starter, there could be a narrow path back to relevance.

Ripple Effects on Depth Charts

Beyond the headline cuts, the 2026 roster reductions have created several dynasty-relevant depth chart shifts. When a team cuts a backup running back, the remaining backs see their handcuff value increase. When a wide receiver is released, the remaining receivers in that offense get a target share boost. These second-order effects are where sharp dynasty managers find edges. Check the Fantasy Draft Pros trade analyzer to see how player values have shifted in response to the latest roster moves.

Waiver Wire Targets After Roster Cuts

The flip side of roster cuts is opportunity. Players who were buried on deep rosters sometimes get cut and sign with teams where they can contribute immediately. Keep an eye on the waiver wire for cut players landing in favorable situations — a receiver who was WR6 on a loaded roster might become WR3 on a pass-heavy team with weaker competition. The dynasty waiver wire is one of the most underutilized tools in fantasy football, and post-cut season is the best time to find league-winning additions for free.

How to React to Roster Cuts in Dynasty

The most important thing is to not overreact. A player being cut doesn't always mean their career is over — landing spot matters enormously. Use the Fantasy Draft Pros trade analyzer to evaluate any trade offers that come in for players affected by roster cuts. If a panicking manager in your league is selling a cut player for pennies on the dollar and that player has a realistic path to a new team, that's a buying opportunity. Conversely, if you're holding a cut player with no clear future, don't be afraid to drop them for a prospect with more upside. Dynasty success is about constant roster optimization.
