Yahoo Fantasy Football League Import

Yahoo Fantasy Football is one of the largest and most established fantasy platforms, hosting millions of leagues every season. But when it comes to advanced trade analysis and dynasty roster evaluation, Yahoo's built-in tools fall short. Fantasy Draft Pros bridges that gap with a seamless Yahoo league import feature that pulls in your entire roster and gives you instant access to dynasty trade values, roster grades, power rankings, and AI-driven trade recommendations tailored specifically to your team.

Yahoo's native trade evaluator provides basic projected point comparisons that only look at the current season. It does not factor in dynasty value, age, positional scarcity, or league format nuances like Superflex and TEP scoring. Fantasy Draft Pros takes your Yahoo roster and applies a dynasty-first valuation model that considers long-term player trajectories, draft pick values, and format-specific adjustments. The result is a complete picture of your roster's strength and the smartest moves you can make to improve it.

How to Import Your Yahoo League

Getting your Yahoo league into Fantasy Draft Pros is straightforward. Head to the Rankings section of our platform, select Yahoo as your league platform, and enter your Yahoo league ID. You can find your league ID in the URL of your Yahoo league homepage. Once entered, Fantasy Draft Pros will import all team rosters in your league within seconds. Every player is assigned a current dynasty trade value, and your team receives a positional roster grade so you can immediately see where you are strong and where you need to improve.

Roster Grades and Power Rankings

After importing your Yahoo league, Fantasy Draft Pros generates detailed roster grades for every team. Your roster is evaluated at each position group: quarterback, running back, wide receiver, tight end, and flex. Each grade reflects not just current production projections but dynasty asset value, giving you a true sense of your team's competitiveness both now and in future seasons. The league-wide power rankings feature stacks every team against each other based on total roster value. You can instantly see who the top contenders are, which teams are rebuilding, and where the trade opportunities lie.

The trade suggestion engine takes your roster data and identifies specific deals you should be targeting. If your roster is weak at running back but loaded at wide receiver, Fantasy Draft Pros will flag potential trades with league mates who have the opposite roster composition. These suggestions include full value breakdowns so you know exactly what to offer and what to expect in return. Whether you are in a dynasty, keeper, or redraft Yahoo league, our import tool gives you the analytical edge you need to dominate your league.
