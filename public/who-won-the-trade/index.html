Why Trade Verdicts Matter

Fantasy football leagues are won and lost on the trade market. A single lopsided deal can shift the balance of power for an entire season — or in dynasty, for years. The problem is that most managers evaluate trades based on gut feeling or name recognition. They overpay for aging stars and undervalue young breakout candidates. Our trade analyzer removes that bias entirely.
