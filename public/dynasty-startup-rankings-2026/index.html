Dynasty Startup Rankings 2026 — Your Complete Draft Order Guide

Starting a new dynasty league in 2026? Your startup draft is the single most important event in your league's history. The players you select in the first three rounds will define your team for years to come. Unlike redraft, dynasty startup drafts require you to balance current production with long-term upside — a 22-year-old ascending talent is often worth more than a 28-year-old putting up similar numbers today. Here are the Fantasy Draft Pros top 10 dynasty startup rankings for 2026, based on our proprietary trade value system.

Top 10 Dynasty Startup Rankings — 2026

1. Bijan Robinson (RB, ATL) — Value: 9,999

Bijan Robinson is the consensus 1.01 in every dynasty startup draft and it's not particularly close. At just 24 years old, Robinson combines elite rushing efficiency, receiving ability, and volume in Atlanta's offense. He's the rare running back who transcends positional value concerns — his production floor is RB1 and his ceiling is the overall fantasy RB1 for the next five-plus years. You don't think twice about this pick.

2. Ja'Marr Chase (WR, CIN) — Value: 9,980

Ja'Marr Chase has established himself as the best wide receiver in fantasy football. His connection with Joe Burrow is arguably the best QB-WR duo in the NFL, and Chase is entering the prime of his career at 26. His combination of contested-catch ability, route precision, and big-play explosiveness makes him a locked-in WR1 for the foreseeable future. In non-Superflex formats, Chase is a legitimate 1.01 argument.

3. Jahmyr Gibbs (RB, DET) — Value: 9,964

Jahmyr Gibbs has taken over as the lead back in Detroit's high-powered offense and his dynasty value reflects it. At 23, Gibbs offers elite receiving upside paired with increasingly dominant rushing numbers. The Lions' offensive line is one of the best in football, and Gibbs' role has expanded every season. He's a top-three dynasty asset regardless of format.

4. Jaxon Smith-Njigba (WR, SEA) — Value: 9,400

JSN's breakout has been everything dynasty managers hoped for. The 23-year-old has emerged as Seattle's alpha receiver with elite separation skills and a target volume that makes him a weekly fantasy anchor. His youth and ascending trajectory make him a premium startup pick.

5. Puka Nacua (WR, LAR) — Value: 8,800

Puka Nacua's historic start to his career has only continued into his third season. At 25, Nacua is Matthew Stafford's clear number-one target and a PPR monster. His ability to win from the slot and outside gives him scheme-proof upside that translates to any offensive system.

6. Josh Allen (QB, BUF) — Value: 8,850 (Superflex)

In Superflex formats, Josh Allen is the top quarterback off the board. His rushing upside creates a fantasy floor that no other QB can match, and his passing efficiency continues to improve. Allen is 30 but quarterbacks age far more gracefully than skill position players — he could be a top-five fantasy QB for another six or seven years.

7. Brock Bowers (TE, LV) — Value: 8,350

Brock Bowers broke the rookie tight end records and hasn't slowed down. At just 23, Bowers is already a top-three fantasy tight end with a positional advantage that's nearly impossible to replicate. Tight end takes years to develop for most players — Bowers skipped the learning curve entirely. His dynasty value reflects generational tight end talent.

8. Malik Nabers (WR, NYG) — Value: 8,145

Malik Nabers has the talent to be the WR1 overall in any given week. The 22-year-old is a dynamic playmaker with elite after-the-catch ability. His quarterback situation adds some risk, but Nabers' talent transcends it. In dynasty, you draft the talent and trust that the situation will improve.

9. Justin Jefferson (WR, MIN) — Value: 8,032

Justin Jefferson remains one of the best route runners in football and a perennial WR1. At 27, he's squarely in his prime with Sam Darnold proving to be a capable passer in Minnesota's system. Jefferson's floor is incredibly safe and his ceiling remains elite.

10. Amon-Ra St. Brown (WR, DET) — Value: 7,900

ARSB is the PPR king. His target volume in Detroit's pass-heavy offense makes him one of the safest weekly plays in fantasy. At 26, St. Brown is entering his peak years with a quarterback in Jared Goff who feeds him relentlessly. He's a startup steal if he falls past pick 10.

Dynasty Startup Draft Strategy Tips

In your startup draft, prioritize young elite talent over aging stars. A 23-year-old WR2 is often more valuable than a 29-year-old WR1 because you're buying years of production, not just one season. Don't reach for quarterbacks in 1QB leagues — wait until rounds 5-7 where the value gap between QB6 and QB15 is minimal. In Superflex, grab your first QB by round 3 at the latest.
