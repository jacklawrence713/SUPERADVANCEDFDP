FAAB Strategy and Waiver Priority

If your league uses FAAB (Free Agent Acquisition Budget), knowing how much to bid on a waiver target is just as important as identifying the right player. Overbid and you waste budget you will need later in the season. Underbid and you lose the player to a league mate who valued him more aggressively. The key is understanding the difference between a league-winning pickup and a one-week rental. A backup running back inheriting 20 touches per game for the rest of the season is worth a significant chunk of your FAAB. A receiver who caught two touchdowns on four targets in a blowout probably is not worth more than a few dollars.

Types of Waiver Wire Targets

The best waiver wire pickups generally fall into three categories. First, handcuffs who inherit full workloads due to injury — these are the most obvious and most contested adds. Second, breakout players who are earning expanded roles through strong practice and game performance — these are harder to spot early but can be league-winners if you get ahead of the curve. Third, streaming options for quarterback, tight end, and defense based on weekly matchups.
