How Keeper Trade Values Work

In keeper leagues, a player's trade value depends on more than just talent. The round you drafted them in, how many years of keeper eligibility remain, and positional scarcity all matter. Fantasy Draft Pros factors in age curves, contract length, and draft capital cost so you can evaluate keeper trades with confidence.
