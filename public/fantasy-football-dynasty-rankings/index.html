How Our Dynasty Rankings Work

Every player receives an FDP dynasty value from 0 to 9,999. Rankings are driven by age-adjusted production curves — younger players at scarce positions receive the biggest boosts. Values shift based on your league's scoring format: PPR, Half PPR, Standard, Superflex, and TE Premium all produce different rankings.
