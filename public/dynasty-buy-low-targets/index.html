Dynasty Buy Low Targets 2026 — Undervalued Players to Acquire Now

Smart dynasty managers know that championships are won in the offseason. The key to building a dominant roster is identifying buy low targets — elite talent whose trade value has dipped below their true production ceiling. Whether it's a down year, an injury recovery, or a coaching change creating uncertainty, these windows don't stay open forever. Here are the top dynasty buy low targets heading into the 2026 NFL season, with current trade values from Fantasy Draft Pros.

Marvin Harrison Jr. (WR, ARI) — Value: 5,500

MHJ's rookie season didn't live up to the astronomical hype, and that's exactly why he's a screaming buy right now. His dynasty value has dropped to 5,500 — well below where a 23-year-old wide receiver with generational talent should sit. Harrison still profiles as a future WR1 with elite route-running, contested catch ability, and a quarterback situation that should only improve. Managers who panicked after year one are selling at a discount you may never see again. Acquire him before the 2026 season reminds everyone why he was the WR1 pick.

Hunter Henry (TE, NE) — Value: 4,400

Tight end is the hardest position to fill in dynasty, and Hunter Henry at 4,400 represents significant value. Henry has been one of the most consistent pass-catching tight ends in the NFL, and his target share in New England's offense makes him a reliable TE1. The tight end landscape is top-heavy, and Henry's price is far below what you'd pay for comparable weekly production at the position. If you need tight end help, this is one of the best values available.

Breece Hall (RB, NYJ) — Value: 5,600

Breece Hall remains one of the most talented young backs in the league, but his dynasty value has settled at 5,600 due to the Jets' overall offensive struggles. Hall is still just 24 years old with elite receiving ability and breakaway speed. Running backs with his skill set and age profile typically command values north of 7,000. A change of scenery or offensive line improvement could catapult his production — and his value — back to the elite tier. Buy him now while the discount lasts.

Anthony Richardson (QB, IND) — Value: 6,800

In Superflex and 2QB leagues, Anthony Richardson at 6,800 is one of the biggest buy-low opportunities in dynasty. His rushing upside gives him a fantasy ceiling that only a handful of quarterbacks can match. Yes, he's had durability concerns and inconsistent passing, but he's still developing and the physical tools are otherworldly. If Richardson puts together a full healthy season, his value could easily double. The risk-reward profile here is heavily tilted in your favor.

Chris Olave (WR, NO) — Value: 6,300

Chris Olave has been one of the most efficient route runners in the NFL since entering the league, yet his dynasty value sits at just 6,300. Quarterback volatility in New Orleans has suppressed his numbers, but Olave's talent has never been the question. He's a polished technician who wins at all three levels of the field and is entering his prime years. When the Saints stabilize at quarterback — whether through the draft, free agency, or a trade — Olave's production will explode. His current price doesn't reflect his long-term ceiling.
