Dynasty Sell High Targets 2026 — Maximize Value Before It's Too Late

In dynasty fantasy football, knowing when to sell is just as important as knowing when to buy. The best dynasty managers extract maximum value from aging or peaking players before their production — and trade value — falls off a cliff. The 2026 season presents several clear sell high opportunities where you can flip current production for long-term dynasty assets. Here are the players you should be shopping aggressively right now.

Derrick Henry (RB, BAL) — Value: 4,500

Derrick Henry defied Father Time with a monster 2024 season in Baltimore, but the clock is ticking louder than ever. At 32 years old, Henry's dynasty value of 4,500 still reflects his name recognition and recent production — but running backs this age historically fall off suddenly and without warning. Henry's rushing style relies on physicality and volume, both of which become liabilities as the body breaks down. If you can flip Henry for a young asset or a package of picks, do it now. His value will never be higher than it is today. The 2026 season could easily be the one where the decline finally hits.

Saquon Barkley (RB, PHI) — Value: 5,450

Saquon Barkley's resurgence in Philadelphia has been remarkable, but at 5,450, his dynasty value is propped up by a situation that may not last. Barkley is 29 years old and has an extensive injury history including a torn ACL. The Eagles' elite offensive line has been masking some of the explosive athleticism that's naturally fading with age. Barkley's current value lets you get a legitimate young piece in return — maybe a rising sophomore receiver or a future first-round pick. Contending teams in your league will pay a premium for his short-term production. Let them.

Aaron Jones (RB, MIN) — Value: 2,711

Aaron Jones at 2,711 might not seem like a sell-high candidate at first glance, but consider the context: he's 31 years old and one bad game away from his value cratering into the hundreds. If Jones is on your roster and you're not a serious contender this year, there is zero reason to hold him. His value will only go down from here. Flip him to a contending team for a late pick or a young depth piece. Even getting a 2027 third-round pick in return is better than watching his value evaporate on your bench over the next twelve months.

Travis Kelce (TE, KC) — Value: 2,800

Travis Kelce is the greatest tight end in NFL history, but he's also 36 years old heading into 2026. His dynasty value of 2,800 still carries weight because of his name and the Chiefs' offense, but the production has been declining each of the past two seasons. Kelce's target share has shrunk as Kansas City has diversified its passing attack, and retirement rumors swirl every offseason. If a league mate still views Kelce as a top-five tight end, take advantage of that perception gap immediately. You can likely get a younger tight end plus a pick in return — assets that will matter far longer than Kelce's remaining career.
