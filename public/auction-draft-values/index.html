Auction Draft Values 2026 — Dollar Values for Every Player

Auction drafts are the purest form of fantasy football drafting. Unlike snake drafts where your pick order is predetermined, auction drafts give every manager an equal shot at every player. The catch? You have a limited budget, and every dollar you spend on one player is a dollar you cannot spend elsewhere. Knowing each player's fair market value before you enter the draft room is the single biggest edge you can have. Fantasy Draft Pros provides complete 2026 auction draft values with dollar amounts for every fantasy-relevant player across all positions.

Our auction values are calibrated for a standard $200 budget in 10-team and 12-team leagues, with adjustments available for different budget sizes and league configurations. Each player's dollar value is derived from their projected fantasy points relative to replacement-level production at their position. This value-over-replacement approach ensures that your auction dollars are allocated efficiently — you pay for the production that actually wins matchups, not for baseline points you could get from the waiver wire for free.

Auction Draft Strategy: Stars and Scrubs vs. Balanced

There are two dominant auction draft strategies, and understanding both is critical to success. The "stars and scrubs" approach involves spending 70-80 percent of your budget on three or four elite players and filling the rest of your roster with $1 minimum-bid sleepers. The theory is that elite production is disproportionately valuable and worth concentrating your budget on. The balanced approach spreads your budget more evenly, targeting a roster full of solid mid-tier starters with no glaring weaknesses. Both strategies can win championships, but they require different in-draft discipline. Our auction values help you identify which players are overpriced relative to their projected output and which represent bargains, regardless of which strategy you prefer.

Budget Tracking and Real-Time Adjustments

One of the most important skills in an auction draft is tracking how much money remains in the room. As other managers overspend on popular players early, the remaining player pool becomes increasingly underpriced relative to the available budget. Smart auction drafters recognize when the room is running out of money and adjust their bidding accordingly. If half the league has already blown their budgets on quarterbacks and running backs, the wide receivers nominated later in the draft will go for significant discounts. Fantasy Draft Pros rankings give you a baseline value for every player so you can instantly recognize when someone is going above or below their fair price during the draft.
