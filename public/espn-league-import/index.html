ESPN Fantasy Football League Import

Managing your ESPN fantasy football league just got a whole lot easier. Fantasy Draft Pros lets you import your ESPN league roster directly into our platform for instant analysis, roster grading, and trade recommendations. Whether you play in a dynasty, keeper, or redraft league on ESPN, our import tool pulls in your full roster and assigns dynasty trade values to every player so you can see exactly where your team stands and what moves you should be making.

ESPN is one of the most popular fantasy football platforms in the world, but its built-in trade tools leave a lot to be desired. The ESPN trade analyzer is basic at best, offering little more than projected point comparisons for the current week. It does not account for dynasty value, age curves, Superflex scoring, or long-term roster building. Fantasy Draft Pros fills that gap by layering advanced dynasty analytics on top of your existing ESPN roster. Once imported, you can see roster grades by position, identify weaknesses, and get AI-powered trade suggestions tailored to your team composition.

How to Import Your ESPN League

Importing your ESPN league into Fantasy Draft Pros takes just a few steps. Navigate to the Rankings tab on our platform, select your league platform as ESPN, and enter your league ID. You can find your ESPN league ID in the URL when you visit your league page on ESPN. Our system will pull in all team rosters, assign current dynasty values, and generate power rankings for every team in your league. The entire process takes under thirty seconds and gives you a comprehensive view of your league landscape that ESPN simply does not provide.

What You Get After Import

Once your ESPN league is imported, Fantasy Draft Pros unlocks several powerful features. You will see a roster grade for your team broken down by quarterback, running back, wide receiver, tight end, and flex positions. Each player on your roster is assigned a current dynasty value so you can quickly identify your most and least valuable assets. The power rankings feature ranks every team in your league from strongest to weakest based on total roster value, giving you insight into which teams are contenders and which are in rebuild mode. You can also use the trade analyzer to test specific deals using your actual roster players, making it easy to evaluate incoming offers or craft proposals that your league mates will accept.
