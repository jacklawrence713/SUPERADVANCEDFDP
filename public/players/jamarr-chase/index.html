Dynasty Value Analysis

Ja'Marr Chase commands a 9,980 dynasty value, making him a top-3 asset in all formats. At age 26, Chase is in his prime (WR prime: 23-29) and producing at a historically elite level. His connection with Joe Burrow and dominant target share in Cincinnati make him a locked-in WR1 for years to come. Chase is one of the safest dynasty holds in the game.

2026 Projected Stats

Chase is projected for an elite 135 receptions, 1,800 yards, and 18 touchdowns in 2026 — truly generational numbers.

Trade Value

Chase is virtually untradeable. Comparable values include Bijan Robinson (9,999) and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (9,998). Only a massive overpay — multiple firsts and a young star — should pry Chase from your roster.
