Dynasty Value Analysis

Joe Burrow holds a 5,722 dynasty value as an elite pocket passer. At 30, Burrow is in his prime (QB prime: 26-35) with five more peak years ahead. When healthy, Burrow is a top-3 fantasy QB with elite volume and TD upside. His connection with Ja'Marr Chase makes the Bengals' passing attack one of the best in football. Health history is the only concern.

2026 Projected Stats

Burrow is projected for 4,800 passing yards and 39 touchdowns in 2026 when healthy — elite QB1 numbers.

Trade Value

Burrow is a strong contender QB. Comparable values include Caleb Williams (5,653) and Saquon Barkley (5,604). In superflex, he is worth a first plus a starter. Health discount keeps his value below younger QBs, but the ceiling is massive.
