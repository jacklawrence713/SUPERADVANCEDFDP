Dynasty Value Analysis

Christian McCaffrey holds a dynasty value of 5,700 — still a premium asset despite being past his prime (RB prime: 22-27). Contenders should hold for a title push, while rebuilders should sell now.

2026 Projected Stats

McCaffrey is projected for approximately 1,100 rushing yards and 68 receptions in 2026. His PPR value remains elite when healthy, but durability is the key question mark.

Trade Value
