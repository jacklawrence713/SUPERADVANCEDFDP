Dynasty Value Analysis

Christian McCaffrey holds a dynasty value of 5,700 — still a premium asset despite being past his prime (RB prime: 22-27). At age 30, CMC's value is declining but his dual-threat ability as both a rusher and receiver keeps him relevant as a high-end RB2. Health concerns are the primary risk factor. Contenders should hold for a title push, while rebuilders should sell now.

2026 Projected Stats

McCaffrey is projected for approximately 1,100 rushing yards and 68 receptions in 2026. His PPR value remains elite when healthy, but durability is the key question mark.

Trade Value

As a veteran RB with elite production history, McCaffrey still commands trade value. Comparable values include Tee Higgins (5,716) and Kyren Williams (5,667). Sellers can likely get a late first-round pick from a contender.
