Dynasty Value Analysis

Patrick Mahomes holds a dynasty value of 6,550 — still a premier quarterback in Fantasy Draft Pros rankings. At age 31, Mahomes is in his prime (QB prime: 26-35), meaning he has multiple elite years remaining. His consistent 4,500+ yard, 40+ TD production and championship pedigree make him one of the safest dynasty QB assets in the game.

2026 Projected Stats

Mahomes is projected for approximately 4,600 passing yards and 40 touchdowns in 2026. His elite floor and ceiling make him a top-5 QB in all formats.

Trade Value

As the game's premier quarterback, Mahomes commands strong trade value. Comparable values include Jalen Hurts (5,489) and Matthew Stafford (5,553). Moving Mahomes should require multiple first-round picks — he is a franchise cornerstone.
