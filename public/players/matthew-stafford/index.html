Dynasty Value Analysis

Matthew Stafford holds a dynasty value of 5,553 — still a strong asset despite being past his prime (QB prime: 26-35). At age 38, Stafford's dynasty value is declining but his production as a veteran starter in LA's offense remains relevant. He is a win-now QB asset with limited long-term dynasty upside.

2026 Projected Stats

Stafford is projected for approximately 3,600 passing yards and 24 touchdowns in 2026. His steady production makes him a backend QB1 for contending teams.

Trade Value

As a veteran QB, Stafford has moderate trade value. Comparable values include Patrick Mahomes (5,544) and Jalen Hurts (5,489). Contenders may pay a second-round pick for his production, while rebuilders should sell immediately.
