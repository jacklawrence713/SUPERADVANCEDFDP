Dynasty Value Analysis

Derrick Henry holds a dynasty value of 4,500 — still a strong asset despite being well past his prime (RB prime: 22-27). At age 32, Henry's dynasty value is declining but his sheer rushing volume in Baltimore's run-heavy offense keeps him relevant as an RB1. His age creates significant risk for a cliff year. Contenders should hold for a title push, while rebuilders must sell immediately.

2026 Projected Stats

Henry is projected for approximately 1,600 rushing yards and 12 touchdowns in 2026. His workhorse volume keeps his floor high despite the age risk.

Trade Value

As an aging workhorse RB, Henry has moderate trade value. Comparable values include D.J. Moore (4,783) and Jared Goff (4,577). Sellers should target a late first or early second from a contender needing a title push.
