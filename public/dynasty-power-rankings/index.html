Championship Odds and Roster Grades

Raw roster value does not tell the whole story. A team loaded with young prospects might have the highest total dynasty value but lack the proven starters needed to compete this season. That is why our power rankings include both a long-term dynasty grade and a short-term contender score. The contender score weights current-year production more heavily, factoring in projected points, bye weeks, and positional depth. Dynasty grade focuses on age-adjusted value and future asset accumulation. Together, these two scores paint a complete picture of where each team stands now and where they are headed.

Use Power Rankings to Drive Trades

The real power of league-wide rankings is identifying trade opportunities. If a rebuilding team has a veteran wide receiver who is clearly surplus to their long-term plan, that is a buy target for contenders. If a contending team is thin at tight end, they might overpay for a top-10 option to shore up their roster for a playoff push. Fantasy Draft Pros power rankings make these imbalances visible so you can craft targeted trade offers that make sense for both sides.
