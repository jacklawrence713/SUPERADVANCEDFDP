Rankings for Every Format

Fantasy football is not one-size-fits-all, and neither are our rankings. In PPR leagues, pass-catching running backs and high-volume slot receivers climb the board. In standard scoring, touchdown-dependent backs and red-zone targets hold more weight. Superflex leagues completely reshape the quarterback landscape — a QB1 in superflex can be worth more than any non-QB on the board. Our rankings engine accounts for all of these nuances automatically. Just select your league format and the rankings adjust in real time.
